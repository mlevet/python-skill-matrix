# Interview Frequency Ranking

Use this to decide what deserves your time. Avoid over-investing in exotic topics before the core ones are solid.

---

## Very High

Topics that appear in almost every advanced Python interview. Master these first.

- Lambda functions
- Closures
- Late binding
- Decorators
- Mutability & default mutable arguments
- Generators & `yield`

---

## High

Topics that appear regularly and signal real Python fluency when explained well.

- Functions as objects
- Higher-order functions (`map`, `filter`, `functools`)
- MRO & `super()`
- `__call__` and callable objects
- Context managers (`with`, `__enter__` / `__exit__`)
- Comprehensions (list, dict, set, generator)
- `*args`, `**kwargs`, positional-only, keyword-only args
- LEGB scoping
- `is` vs `==`
- Iterables vs iterators

---

## Medium

Topics that come up in senior-level or specialist roles, or as follow-ups.

- Descriptors
- `@property`
- `@classmethod` vs `@staticmethod`
- `__new__` vs `__init__`
- `__slots__`
- `functools.partial` and `lru_cache`
- Walrus operator (`:=`)
- Extended unpacking
- `asyncio` & `await`
- Dataclasses

---

## Low

Worth knowing, but rarely the focus of an interview question.

- Metaclasses
- Pattern matching (`match`/`case`)
- `__init_subclass__`
- Bytecode & `dis`
- The GIL
- Import system internals
